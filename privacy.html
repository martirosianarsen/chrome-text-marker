Text Marker ↗

Your words stay yours.

Privacy policy · Updated October 30, 2026 · Version 1.1.0

What is saved

When you highlight text, Text Marker stores the selected passage, its color, the page URL and title, the creation time, and nearby text and position needed to find it again. These records remain in Chrome's local extension storage on your device.

What leaves your device

Text Marker does not send your highlights or browsing activity to the developer or third parties. It contains no analytics, advertising, accounts, remote scripts, or tracking. It reads webpage text locally to create and restore highlights, and does not scan editable fields. An exported JSON file contains your saved data and is downloaded only when you choose Export. Opening a saved page or the GitHub support link makes an ordinary browser request to that website, whose own privacy policy applies.

Permissions

Storage keeps your highlights on your device. Active tab lets the popup identify the page you are viewing after you click the extension. The content script runs on HTTP and HTTPS webpages to show the color picker and automatically restore saved highlights. Chrome's restricted pages and built-in PDF viewer are not supported.

Your control

Remove individual highlights or clear a page in the popup. Use All pages to find and remove records from other pages. You can also export a copy. Uninstalling the extension removes its local Chrome storage. Exported files remain until you delete them yourself. There is no cloud backup or automatic sync.

Contact

Questions or requests: GitHub issues. Please do not include private highlighted text in public issues.
